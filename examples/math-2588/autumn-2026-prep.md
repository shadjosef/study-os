# Autumn 2026 exam preparation

Open **Exams → Mathematics → Autumn 2026 Mathematics Mock** for a three-hour paper with 12 equally weighted problems. The questions combine the standard skills practised in class at an intermediate level. Write down the reasoning for each answer. After submitting, use the worked solutions and marking checklists to check your written work.

## What the available 2026 materials cover

| Current source | Skills to practise | In the mock |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lecture 1 and the 19 August exercise session | Simplify fractions and algebraic expressions. Track excluded values before cancelling. | 5–7 |
| Lectures 17–18 and the 28 October exercise session | Read signs of derivatives, distinguish stationary points from extrema and optimise in one variable. | 1, 4, 9 |
| Lectures 19–20 sheet dated 30 October | Select derivative rules, apply the mean value theorem, differentiate implicitly and explain how curvature changes. | 1–4 |

The other problems sample finance, inverse functions, elasticity and Taylor or limit methods from the autumn reference and older course materials. The lecture PDFs labelled 21–24 available for this review carry 2025 dates. Check the current Canvas announcements for the complete assessed scope and permitted aids.
