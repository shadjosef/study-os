Terms of Use

Independent student project · Last updated November 2026

1. Independent student project

Study-OS (studyos.no) is an independent, non-commercial student study tool and is not officially affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by BI Norwegian Business School, UiA, NTNU, UiO, NHH, UiB, OsloMet, or Instructure / Canvas.

2. Copyright, Bring-Your-Own-PDF & student-synced materials

  • Public practice drills, synthetic exam questions, FSRS schedules, and study summaries included in Study-OS are original study aids.
  • No public hosting of copyrighted textbooks: Study-OS does not publicly host or distribute copyrighted publisher textbooks. When you attach your own textbook or lecture PDF locally, it stays 100% private inside your own browser (IndexedDB) and is never uploaded to Study-OS servers.
  • If you connect your own institutional Canvas, TimeEdit, TP, or Ed Discussion account using the local Desktop Edition or browser extension, you are responsible for ensuring you only access materials you are authorized to view as an enrolled student and for never uploading copyrighted textbooks, slides, or exam PDFs to public community sets or messages.

3. Academic integrity & active assessments

Study-OS is strictly for self-study and exam preparation. Never post, share, request, or use Study-OS Community, Study Rooms, or the AI Tutor with live/active graded exams, home exams, or restricted assessment materials.

4. Fair use & community

Do not abuse the hosted AI Tutor, spam community courses, attempt to bypass authentication/rate limits, or impersonate other students. Accounts violating these rules may be restricted or removed.

5. As-is disclaimer

Study-OS, AI Tutor hints, estimated exam readiness indicators, GPA calculations, and study schedules are provided "as is" for personal study support without warranty of accuracy, uninterrupted availability, or exam outcomes. Always verify official deadlines, grades, and syllabus rules on your university's official portal.

6. Copyright / Takedown Notices & Contact

We respect intellectual property rights under Norwegian copyright law (Åndsverkloven) and international law (including DMCA notice-and-takedown). If you are a lecturer, university representative, publisher, or rights holder and believe any material on studyos.no infringes your rights, please email shad.josef@outlook.com with the URL or item description and your contact details. Valid takedown requests are reviewed and acted on promptly (typically within 24–48 hours).

General questions, bug reports, or feedback can also be emailed to shad.josef@outlook.com or submitted at github.com/shadjosef/study-os.
