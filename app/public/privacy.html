Study-OS

Privacy Policy

Independent student project · Last updated November 2026

Study-OS is a free, independent student study workspace built with privacy by default. You can practice directly in your browser without creating an account.

1. Data stored locally on your device

Practice answers, timers, theme preferences, study schedules, and optional API keys (if you enter your own Google Gemini key) are stored locally in your browser's localStorage and stay on your device unless you sign in to sync them.

2. Optional accounts & Google Sign-In

  • If you choose to sign in with Google, Passkey, or a study profile, Study-OS stores your display name, account identifier, and synced study progress on Cloudflare Workers / D1 (EU region).
  • Google Sign-In only verifies your identity (basic OpenID profile / email). Study-OS does not sell your data or access your Gmail, Drive, or other Google services.
  • Study-OS's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Optional Browser Sync & AI Tutor

  • The optional Study-OS Chrome extension only runs when you click it on a signed-in BI Canvas, TimeEdit, or Ed Discussion tab. Course names, module titles, and deadlines you choose to sync are saved to your account; community course directories only merge public course codes, titles, and topic outlines—never private grades, submissions, or copyrighted files.
  • When you use the built-in AI Tutor, your prompt and the current question context are sent to the configured model provider (Cloudflare Workers AI or Google Gemini) solely to generate your tutor response.

4. Cookies & tracking

Study-OS uses no advertising networks, no third-party analytics trackers, and no cross-site tracking cookies. Only strictly necessary browser storage is used to keep your session and local preferences working.

5. Your rights & contact

Under GDPR (Norway / EEA), you can export your data, sign out and clear local storage at any time, disconnect Study-OS from your Google Account, or request deletion of your synced account data via the project repository at github.com/shadjosef/study-os.
